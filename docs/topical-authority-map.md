## Link rules for the next implementation

- Add one link only when the surrounding paragraph has the same decision boundary as the destination. Do not place a service link in a list of unrelated articles or at the end of every page.
- Use the visible service title as the anchor where it reads naturally. Keep copy short and specific to call-center work in the Philippines.
- Each edited paragraph must remain two or three complete sentences. It should explain why the service page answers the reader's next question.
- Keep decisions about refunds, account changes, legal matters, payment handling, and policy exceptions with the named client manager. Do not let service copy imply otherwise.
- Before a public change, verify the source URL, generated route, sitemap entry, and the exact public marker on the apex and `www` host. The source map itself has no public route.
